Nuvicon Delivery Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver only within the delivery area shown in the app. When you choose your delivery location on the map, the app shows the distance from our godown and tells you if the location is outside the delivery area.

Delivery time

  • Orders are accepted during store hours shown in the app.
  • We prepare your order at our godown and a delivery partner brings it to your location. You can follow the status (Placed, Staged, Picked up, In transit, Delivered) under Orders in the app.
  • Delivery time depends on the product, quantity and distance. We will tell you the expected time when your order is confirmed.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges, if any, depend on the distance and the delivery partner, and are confirmed when your order is confirmed.

At the time of delivery

  • Please be available on the phone number you gave us and check the products on arrival.
  • For cash on delivery orders, pay the amount to the delivery partner. Credit customers are not asked to pay on delivery.
