Nuvicon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Nuvicon ("we", "us") lets you order construction materials for delivery. This policy explains what information the Nuvicon app collects, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Phone number – used to log you in with a one-time password (OTP) and to contact you about your order.
  • Name – if you give it, to identify your orders.
  • Delivery address and location – the address you type, and the map location you choose or share, so we can calculate distance and deliver your order. We access your device location only when you tap "My location".
  • Order details – items, quantities, prices, payment method, order status and history.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, or browsing history, and we do not show ads.

How we use it

  • To log you in, take and fulfil your orders, and calculate delivery distance and charges.
  • To share your name, phone number and delivery address/location with the delivery partner assigned to your order, only so they can deliver it.
  • To keep records for accounting, support and preventing misuse.

Who processes your data

We use trusted service providers to run the app: Supabase (database and login, servers in Mumbai, India), Vercel (hosting), Google Maps Platform (maps, address search and distance), and an SMS provider (to send OTPs). They process data only to provide these services. We do not sell your personal information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to order data is limited to our staff and the delivery partner assigned to your order.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and order records while your account is active and as long as needed for business and legal records. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time by emailing us from the contact address below with your registered phone number. We will act on it within a reasonable time; some order records may be retained where the law requires.

Children

Nuvicon is meant for adults placing business or household material orders and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page with its date.
