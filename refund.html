Nuvicon Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Cancelling an order

  • You can ask us to cancel an order before it is dispatched from our godown. Contact us with your order number.
  • If an online payment was not completed, you can cancel the order in the app under Orders.
  • Once an order is picked up for delivery it cannot be cancelled.

Refunds

  • If you paid online and the order is cancelled by you before dispatch, or by us (for example, stock is not available), we refund the full amount to the original payment method.
  • Refunds are usually completed within 5 to 7 working days after the cancellation, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Cash on delivery orders need no refund if the order is cancelled before delivery.

Damaged or wrong products

  • Please check the products when they are delivered. If an item is damaged or is not what you ordered, tell the delivery partner and contact us the same day with your order number.
  • After we check the issue we will replace the item or refund its price.

Returns

Construction materials such as cement, sand and steel cannot be returned once delivered and accepted, except in the cases above.
